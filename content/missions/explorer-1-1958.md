# explorer-1-1958

## The mission

The United States' answer to Sputnik, Explorer 1 reached orbit on 31 January 1958 after a failed first attempt months earlier. Its cosmic-ray detector, built by James Van Allen's team, returned data showing far fewer particles than expected. The instrument had saturated from an intense radiation belt, and that reading led to the discovery of what's now called the Van Allen radiation belts.
