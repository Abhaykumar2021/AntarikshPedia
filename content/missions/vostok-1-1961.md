# vostok-1-1961

## The mission

On 12 April 1961, Soviet cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space, completing a single orbit of Earth aboard Vostok 1 in 108 minutes. He ejected and parachuted separately from the capsule during descent, per Soviet spacecraft design at the time, though this detail was concealed for years to satisfy record-keeping rules requiring the pilot land with the craft.
